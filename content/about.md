{{< dictionary word="bricoleur" pronunciation="bree-koh-LUR" pos="noun" >}}
Someone who tinkers at home, often without fully knowing what they’re doing or using the right tools, but somehow making it work anyway.
{{< /dictionary >}}

During the day, I work as an engineer for a company that specializes in industrial heating. After hours, I trade the world of boilers and burners for the world of 3D printing, electronics, woodworking, or whatever catches my attention that week.

When I’m working on a project, people sometimes call me a “bricoleur”. They’re not wrong, but I’m also an engineer. Hence the name: Bricoleering.

I’ve always liked making things. All sorts of things. What I enjoy most is turning an idea into something real. Most of my projects start with a random thought, or an inexplicable urge to try something new. Some of the things I’ve made I still use every day. Others I’ve barely even touched. But really, it’s more about the making itself than the end result.

With this site, I want to collect those ideas and document where they take me. Primarily for myself, but anyone curious is welcome to read along. Don’t expect polished tutorials, but rather a record of experiments, failures and my ongoing curiosity.
