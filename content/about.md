Someone who makes, fixes and invents things with whatever happens to be lying around — often without quite knowing what they’re doing, but somehow making it work anyway.

## Welcome to my blog!

This is where I document my bricolage projects — reviving vintage electronics, building tools from scratch, and turning scrap materials into something useful (or at least interesting). Expect a mix of finished builds, half-baked experiments, and the occasional very questionable engineering decision.
